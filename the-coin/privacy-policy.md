## 1. Summary

**In the App's current release, we do not require you to create an account, and the App does not send any personal data to our servers.** All your game progress (coin flip statistics, unlocked cosmetic items, settings) is stored **only on your own device** and is never sent to us. The only exceptions are a small automatic check for updates to these legal documents, which goes to our website host (§2.6), and emails you choose to send us (§2.7).

Some features described below (signing in, cloud save, in-app purchases, analytics) are **not enabled in the current release** but are documented here because we may enable them in a future update — if and when we do, this policy, and the "Data safety" section of the App's Google Play Store listing, will be updated to accurately reflect what is actually active, and, where legally required, we will ask for your consent before that feature starts processing personal data.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1. Data stored only on your device (current release)

- **Game progress**: virtual currency balance, unlocked/equipped cosmetic coin skins and backgrounds, toss statistics (counts, streaks). Stored locally in the App's private storage area on your device. Never transmitted to us.
- **Preferences**: e.g., sound on/off. Stored locally on your device.

This data is not personal data we process, because we never receive it — it stays on your device, under your control, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

### 2.2. Data we would collect if you use optional account features (not enabled in the current release)

If a future update enables signing in (e.g., with a Google account) to sync your progress across devices, we would collect:

- **Account identifier** (a unique ID for your account) and, if you sign in with a third-party provider, the **name and email address** that provider shares with us, so we can identify your account and show it back to you.
- **Cloud-synced game progress** (the same categories as §2.1), so it can be restored on another device.

Legal basis: performance of a contract with you (providing the account/sync feature you requested) and, where applicable, your consent.

### 2.3. Data we would collect if you make an in-app purchase (not enabled in the current release)

If a future update enables purchasing virtual currency with real money, the purchase itself is handled by the app store (e.g., Google Play) under its own privacy policy — we do not see or store your payment card details. We would receive from the app store: a purchase/transaction identifier and the product purchased, so we can verify the purchase and grant the corresponding virtual currency, and a record that the transaction occurred, to prevent fraud and duplicate grants.

Legal basis: performance of a contract with you (delivering what you paid for) and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud.

### 2.4. Data we would collect via analytics/crash reporting (not enabled in the current release)

If a future update enables analytics or crash reporting, we would collect technical data such as device type, operating system version, app version, general (non-precise) crash/error logs, and coarse, aggregated usage events (e.g., "shop opened"), to help us fix bugs and understand which features are used.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the App working correctly and improving it, or your consent, depending on the tool used and your jurisdiction's requirements.

### 2.5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: your real name (unless you choose to sign in with a provider that shares it), precise location, contacts, photos, microphone/camera data, or any government ID data. The App does not contain ads or ad-tracking SDKs as of the effective date above.

### 2.6. Automatic network requests (current release)

- **Legal-document version check.** Each time you open the App, it downloads a small file from our website, hosted on GitHub Pages (operated by GitHub, Inc., USA), to check whether these Terms or this Privacy Policy have changed since you last agreed to them. As with any web request, GitHub receives your device's IP address and basic technical details of the request (such as the time and your device's browser/app identifier) and handles them under its own privacy statement. We do not receive or store this data. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in making sure you have agreed to the current version of these documents.
- **Google Play.** On Android, the App connects to the Google Play billing service on your device. No purchases are offered in the current release, and we receive no data from this connection; Google handles it under its own privacy policy.
- **Links you tap.** Links in the App (for example, to these documents or to our Buy Me a Coffee page) open in your browser. The website you visit receives the usual request data and handles it under its own policy.

### 2.7. If you contact us (current release)

If you email us (for example, using "Send feedback" in the App), we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and whatever you write or attach. We use this only to reply to you and to improve the App based on your feedback. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which stores it on our behalf and may process it outside the EEA (see §5).

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering messages sent to us and improving the App.

## 3. How We Use Data

Where any of the above optional features are enabled and you choose to use them, we use the data only to: provide and maintain the feature you're using (account sync, purchases); prevent fraud and abuse; diagnose and fix technical problems; and comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax/accounting records for purchases).

**We do not sell your personal data.** We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

## 4. Who We Share Data With

We do not share personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. In the current release, the only third parties involved are GitHub, which hosts our website and the legal-version file (§2.6), Google Play on your device (§2.6), and Google, which provides our email service (§2.7).

Where a feature described in §2.2–§2.4 is enabled, we share the minimum necessary data with the service providers ("**processors**") who help us operate that feature, for example:

- **Cloud hosting / backend infrastructure** (e.g., Google Firebase / Google Cloud Platform), to store synced account and progress data.
- **Sign-in provider** (e.g., Google Sign-In), to authenticate you.
- **App store billing** (e.g., Google Play Billing), to process purchases.
- **Analytics/crash-reporting provider**, if enabled.

These providers process data on our behalf, under contractual data-processing terms, and only for the purposes we specify. We may also disclose data where required by law, to enforce our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.html), or to protect the rights, property, or safety of DOSLA, our users, or others.

## 5. International Data Transfers

Some service providers we use (see §4) may process data on servers located outside the European Economic Area (EEA), including in the United States. In the current release, this applies to GitHub and Google (§2.6–§2.7), both of which are US companies. Where that occurs, we rely on legally recognized safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an applicable adequacy decision, to protect your data. You may request more detail about the specific safeguard used by contacting us (§10).

## 6. Data Retention

- Locally stored game progress (§2.1) persists until you uninstall the App or clear its data, entirely under your control.
- Emails you send us (§2.7) are kept for as long as we need them to deal with your message, and deleted no later than two years after our last exchange, unless we need them longer to establish or defend a legal claim.
- Where account-based features are enabled, account and cloud-synced progress data (§2.2) are retained for as long as your account is active, and deleted upon a valid deletion request (§8) or, absent a request, after a period of extended inactivity that we will specify once the feature is enabled.
- Purchase transaction records (§2.3) are retained for as long as required by applicable tax and accounting law (generally several years under Slovenian law), even after account deletion, to the extent legally required.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age without appropriate consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data without appropriate consent, contact us (§10) and we will take steps to delete it.

## 8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA (or another jurisdiction granting equivalent rights), you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten"), including via any in-App "Delete my data" feature we provide, where enabled;
- **Restrict** or **object** to certain processing;
- **Data portability** (receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format);
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
- **Lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority — in Slovenia, the Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec), [https://www.ip-rs.si](https://www.ip-rs.si), or the supervisory authority of your own EEA country of residence.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in §10, or use any in-App data-deletion feature we provide. We will respond within the timeframe required by GDPR (generally one month).

**Note on the current release:** the only personal data we hold in the current release is any email you have sent us (§2.7); contact us (§10) to access or delete it. Your game data lives entirely on your device, never on our servers: you can erase it with "Delete local data" in the App's Profile screen, or by uninstalling the App (or clearing its data in your device settings), without needing to contact us.

## 9. Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures (e.g., encrypted connections for any future network features, access controls on our backend) to protect any personal data we process. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
