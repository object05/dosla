It applies automatically; you don't need to sign it. If you want a signed copy, write to hblockdev@gmail.com.

- (c) take the security measures in section 5 (GDPR Art. 32);
- (d) use sub-processors only as section 3 describes;

- (f) help you meet your obligations on security, breach notification, impact assessments and prior consultation (GDPR Art. 32–36), taking into account the information available to us;

- (h) give you the information needed to show compliance with GDPR Art. 28, and allow for and contribute to audits (section 6).

Transfers outside the EEA (to Anthropic in the USA) rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) between us and the sub-processor. We'll give you information about these safeguards on request.

## 5. Security measures

- Hosting in the EU on servers only we administer; databases, admin tools and monitoring not reachable from the internet, only over an encrypted VPN.
- HTTPS for all traffic, enforced with HSTS.
- Access to your content only through your authenticated account, and for our staff only where needed to run or support the Service.
- Passwords stored as Argon2 hashes; short-lived, revocable sessions; rate limits against password guessing.
- Encrypted off-site backups, kept up to 6 months.
- Logging of security events; error tracking and logs self-hosted.
- Anthropic doesn't use our customers' content to train its models under its commercial terms.

## 6. Audits

We'll answer reasonable written questions about our processing and provide our documentation. Where that isn't enough, you may audit us, or have an independent auditor bound by confidentiality do so, once a year, on 30 days' notice, during business hours, at your cost, without access to other customers' data. Authorities may audit as the law requires.

## 7. Deletion

When you delete a survey, or your account, we delete the uploaded document, the extracted text and questions, and the runs straight away from the live systems. Copies leave our backups within 6 months. Our AI sub-processor keeps inputs for a limited time for safety checks, then deletes them.

## 8. Personal data breaches

We'll notify you without undue delay, and aim to do so within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a breach affecting personal data we process for you, with the information you need to meet your own notification duties (GDPR Art. 33). We'll take reasonable steps to contain it.

## 9. Liability and order of precedence

Liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the Terms of Service, as far as the law allows. If this DPA conflicts with the Terms on the processing of personal data, this DPA prevails.
