## 1. What Surveytor is, and what it is not

1.1. Surveytor lets you upload a blank survey and generate **synthetic ("mock") respondents**: invented people whose answers are produced by statistical sampling and by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude). The purpose is to **pre-test a survey's design** (find confusing questions, poor scales, broken skip logic) and get a feel for what the data could look like, before fielding it to real people.

1.2. **Synthetic responses are not real responses.** They don't come from, represent or predict any real person, group or population. They can be wrong, biased, inconsistent or unrealistic. They are not a measurement of opinion, not a sample, and not a substitute for research with real participants.

1.3. **Outputs are AI-generated**, and the Service marks exports as such (EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 50).
